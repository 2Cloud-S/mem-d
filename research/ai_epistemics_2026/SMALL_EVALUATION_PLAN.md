# Smallest report-oriented untouched study — conditional proposal, 2026-11-15

No untouched cases are generated or accessed. This is a smaller alternative to
the Phase 3 100-family proposal, not an already powered experiment. **Do not run
it without an eligible solver, independent evidence/label review, and a complete
pre-answer policy/analysis freeze.** The Phase 3B fixed interface is selected for
interpretability and resources, never a favorable mem-d effect.

Phase 3B completed without a qualified solver: every tested forced-choice
combination passed syntax and failed the 80% accuracy threshold. The proposal
below is conditional planning, not the immediate next execution step. First
complete independent reviews and resolve solver/interface decision eligibility.

Use **24 genuinely new scenario families, two cases each (48 cases)**, balancing
12 cases per existing category. These must be new causal/evidence setups, not
renamed development or calibration templates. Conservatively combine families
sharing an underlying setup or generator. Include eight to sixteen records and
four to eight meaningful distractors per case where suitable, with useful exact
and paraphrase compression opportunities as well as harmful consolidation.
Review labels independently before policy predictions or model responses; do not
select cases by gate approvals, similarity, calibration failures or favorable
expected model answers. Freeze the full family list and planned denominator.

Keep original, exact, unchanged mem-d and the offline similarity-first control
at 0.95; all three paired order seeds. Match actual tokenizer-specific memory
budgets using the existing complete prefix rule and report absolute residuals,
all points, unreachable budgets, actual merges and review burden. No gold-driven
budget choice, new safeguard, gate tuning or silent memory deletion. Freeze
weights/tokenizer, prompt/interface, context limits, settings, code, data, reuse,
scoring/exclusions and family bootstrap before new responses.

For **one qualifying model**, 48 × 3 × 4 = **576 logical observations**, at most
576 physical first attempts (1,728 with two infrastructure retries). Two distinct
qualifying families would require 1,152 logical observations and at most 3,456
attempts, and would support a more useful robustness comparison. Do not count a
second Qwen size as a distinct family. Within-block identity reuse is allowed;
never assume the development reuse fraction or deduplicate orders. With the
24-output-token interface the first-attempt ceiling is 13,824 generated tokens
per model. At 4,096 total tokens, the maximum prompt budget is 2,345,472 tokens
per model; do not truncate longer cases silently. These are conservative bounds,
not measured monetary or total-inference savings.

The two failed small-model calibrations took 1.65 and 1.47 CPU seconds/physical
call on short single-record prompts. They are **not eligible study models** and
those timings cannot be transferred directly to longer inputs or another model.
The failed 1.5B calibration took 5.35 seconds/call on the same short prompts;
576 such calls would take about 51 minutes without reuse. It ran on CPU within
8 GiB, showing that lack of a GPU is not itself the blocker, but its 8/18 accuracy
makes it ineligible for this study. Longer inputs and a different capable model
could require several hours; these throughput figures supply no capability claim.
Measure a qualifying solver's throughput first; reserve provisionally **one CPU
day for one model**, including preparation, longer prefill and possible retries,
instead of requiring a GPU or paying a provider. Budget eight GiB RAM only for
a deployment already demonstrated to fit, plus sufficient local weight/artifact
disk space. A larger or distinct-family model may need more RAM, a verified
quantized CPU backend, or separately authorized inference. Recalibrate after any
backend/quantization change; no unannounced substitution.

Two independent label checks require 96 case reviews: at an illustrative five
minutes each, **eight reviewer-hours**, plus authoring and adjudication. Blinded
actual-medoid proposal audits are additional: one to two proposals/case would
mean 96–192 independent reviewer judgments for two people, roughly eight to
sixteen further hours at five minutes/judgment. These are planning assumptions,
not observed timings. Keep original independent submissions separate. Assistant
authorship or model judgments do not provide independent human validation.

Report original-memory errors and paired introduced/rescued transitions, gate
versus both controls, equal-family error changes and 10,000-resample intervals,
all format failures/exclusions and concentration in families. Retain a five
percentage-point absolute error reduction as a practical minimum effect of
interest, with positive compression and review burden disclosed. Twenty-four
families provide only a **small descriptive evaluation** of these frozen methods,
solver and synthetic distribution. They may reveal failure mechanisms or a
concentrated null result; they cannot establish general AI benefit, equivalence,
or production safety. No reliable power claim follows from the tiny, interface-
sensitive development pilot. Inconclusive intervals must remain inconclusive;
do not extend the sample after viewing the held-out treatment effect.

If no Phase 3B solver qualifies, the immediate report should instead document
the interface/calibration negative finding and pending semantic audits. Obtain
an independently chosen capable solver and rerun the same frozen calibration
under a future bounded amendment before committing author/reviewer resources
to this untouched comparison. An uninterpretable or ineligible solver is not a
reason to generate a bigger dataset.
