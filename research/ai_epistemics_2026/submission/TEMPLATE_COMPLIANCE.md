The template explicitly says its section structure is strong guidance rather than rigid and permits adaptation. We retain Introduction, Related Work, Methods, Results, Discussion and Limitations, Conclusion, Code and Data, References, appendices and LLM Usage Statement. The optional Author Contributions section is omitted rather than inventing a division of work. Unused author cells, “With Apart Research” and all instructional text are removed; the sprint footnote conveys the event connection without asserting an author affiliation.

Layout uses US Letter, one-inch margins, Old Standard TT body text (11 pt), 20 pt title, 14/13 pt main/subheadings, and compact 9–10 pt captions/tables/abstract. Title whitespace and line spacing are adapted for the content. The font family and page geometry follow the actual export. Fonts are embedded in the PDF; the bundled fonts retain their OFL license. This is template-based compliance, not a claim of pixel-identical instructional-page layout.

The current guidelines require a PDF, a 150–250-word abstract, authors/affiliations, no more than eight body pages, and the “Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations” appendix. The template's four-page length is a recommendation; the guidelines recommend four to eight. This report has **six body pages**, one reference page, three appendix pages, a **191-word abstract**, and the required appendix (Appendix A). The LLM Usage Statement is complete and candid about assistant drafting and the absence of independent human semantic validation.

Every final report page was rendered and inspected for clipping, table/plot legibility, overlaps, captions, links and unresolved guidance.
